Solarbloom interface

Sunlit Glass

Frosted warm-white cards over a living world. Leaf green means you can act, sun gold means power, sky blue means water, clay means "not yet". Every button the game asks for is shown as the glyph of the device you are holding.

The world first
Five calm parts while walking. Nothing else unless you open it. Charge is the only meter.
One shell for every panel
Title, tabs, body, action bar. Big panels centre and pause; machine cards dock right so the machine stays in view.
The action bar teaches
Every panel ends with what you can press right now, as glyph and verb. On a pad you read it; with a mouse you click it.
Kind words, few numbers
Status in words ("thirsty"). Counts only for what you gather by hand, as "12 of 20". A "no" always says why.
